01 — The gap

It didn't give you this.

Filtration removes what you don't want. It doesn't add anything back — and it leaves the water's oxidation-reduction potential sitting on the wrong side of zero.

RO strips, it doesn't add

Reverse osmosis leaves water at roughly +150 mV ORP. Clean, but oxidising.

Alkaline bottles need minerals

Plate-only bottles rely on dissolved minerals to electrolyse — so they underperform on RO water, which is what most Indian homes actually drink.

10 — Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

Still unsure? Talk to us before you buy — the 30-day return covers you either way.

Does it work with RO water? +

Yes — tap, RO, filtered or mineral. The SPE/PEM stack splits water at the membrane, so it doesn't need dissolved minerals to electrolyse. That's the main practical difference from plate-only alkaline bottles, which underperform on RO.

How long does the hydrogen stay in the water? +

Drink within 15–20 minutes of the cycle finishing for peak concentration. Hydrogen is the smallest molecule there is and it leaves an open container steadily — keeping the lid on slows it down.

What's the difference between the two cycles? +

Quick Boost runs 3 minutes and reaches about 3,000 PPB. Deep Infusion runs 18 minutes and reaches up to 9,000 PPB with ORP down to −450 mV. Tap the button for the first, hold it for the second.

How many cycles per charge? +

15 or more, depending on which cycle you use. It charges over USB-C in roughly two hours from the same cable as most phones.

Is there any taste or smell? +

Molecular hydrogen is tasteless and odourless. Ozone and chlorine produced on the anode side are exhausted through a separate vent rather than dissolving back into the water.

How do I clean it? +

Rinse with plain water after use and let it air-dry with the lid off. Don't put it in a dishwasher and don't submerge the base — the electronics and the plate stack live there.

What's covered by the warranty? +

365-day replacement on manufacturing defects, plus a 30-day return window if you simply don't get on with it. Shipping across India is free both ways.

Is hydrogen water safe? +

Molecular hydrogen is generally recognised as safe and the device is FDA, GMP, CE and ROHS certified.
